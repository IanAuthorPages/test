What drink takes, and what you get back when you quit

The science says what alcohol does. The stories show what it looks like.

I spent more than twenty years as a therapist. I also spent most of those years as a functioning drinker. So I know the clinical explanation, and I know the 3 a.m. version.

This book gives you both. My stories run all the way through it, because I lived every chapter. But it isn't my life story. It's a plain-English account of what drink does, with real life to show you what that means.

About the author

Ian Bates

Ian spent more than twenty years as a therapist, counselor and hypnotherapist, after serving as a paratrooper and a firefighter. For most of that time he was a functioning drinker: fine on the surface, paying for it every day underneath.

He quit six years ago and hasn't looked back. Today he's teetotal, and he writes plain-talking books about alcohol and the life you get back without it.
